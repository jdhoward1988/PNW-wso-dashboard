Club standings

Scored like a USAW national meet. In each Open weight class the top 25 PNW lifters by total earn 28, 25, 23, 22, 21, 20 and then one point less per place down to 1. As in the USAW Club League, a club's best 5 women and best 5 men count. Ties go to the club with more higher places. Youth clubs have their own table.

#ClubPointsScorersClass wins

Select a club to see who scored. Points come from each lifter's best result, not their place at a particular meet, and each lifter counts once.
