Rank is among PNW WSO lifters only; picking an age group re-ranks within that group. US rank is the lifter's place in USA Weightlifting's national list for the class. Age groups go by the lifter's age at the meet: Youth up to 17, Junior 15–20, Masters 35 and over. A lifter can appear in more than one class if they competed at different bodyweights. USA Weightlifting's public results don't mark adaptive athletes, so the Adaptive badge comes from athletes who opted in with the PNW WSO (approved opt-ins appear after the next Wednesday update); their adaptive category is shown only if they chose to share it. Picking Adaptive re-ranks among adaptive lifters in the class, and picking a category re-ranks within that category, as USAW does at national events. Spot a problem? Results are managed by USA Weightlifting, so corrections go through them.
