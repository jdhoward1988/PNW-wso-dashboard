Draft notes for the board
Data
Nothing here is typed in by hand. Qualifying windows and event dates come from data/qualifying-2027.json, PNW meets, courses and camps from the daily Sport80 check, national events and their registration dates from USAW's pages, and planned sanctioned meets from the Add Your Event form.
Registration
Opening and closing dates are read daily from the dated list on each national event's USAW page (keyDates in data/usaw-national-events.json). Events USAW hasn't given a page yet show “not posted yet”.
Open question
Whether the last day of a window counts. The page treats the closing date as included.
