Qualifying tracker

Which PNW lifters have the total for a 2027 national event, and whose total is a legacy one: big enough, but made before the qualifying window opened, so it has to be done again.

 

  • Loading the rankings…
  • Qualified: the lifter has a total inside this event's window that meets the qualifying total for the class they lifted in that day. Under USAW's rule they may then register in any class.
  • Legacy total: the lifter has made the total, but only before the window opened, so it doesn't count for this event and has to be done again. Every meet result since last season is checked, not just the lifter's best.
  • Class and age group: the class is the one the lifter's bodyweight fell in on the day, using the 2027 classes. The age group uses the age they turn in the event's year, from their birth year. “All events” on a lifter shows the same check for every 2027 event and lists their results.
  • This list is a planning aid, not an entry list. USAW confirms qualification at registration. University totals only apply to enrolled students.
  • Adaptive athletes: lifters who have self-identified through the PNW WSO's adaptive opt-in form are marked Adaptive and checked against USAW's adaptive qualifying total for their class, which is 50% of the standard total, with the same window. To enter a national event as an adaptive athlete USAW also requires its health professional's approval form. Adaptive athletes who haven't opted in are checked against the standard total.
Draft notes for the board
Data
Nothing is typed in by hand. Every meet result since January 1 of last season, and each lifter's birth year, are saved daily to data/results.json from the public results table on each lifter's USAW rankings profile. Qualifying totals are read daily from USAW's own PDF into data/qualifying-2027.json.
Rule used
From USAW's 2027 qualifying totals page: the total must be posted inside the event's qualification period, and a lifter who makes the qualifying total for the class they lifted in can register in that or any other class. A total that misses the standard for the class it was made in can't be used for a lighter class.
Adaptive
The opt-in list is data/adaptive.json, the same one the rankings page uses. Adaptive totals are the ones USAW prints in its qualifying totals PDF (50% of the standard total, rounded by USAW). Only the word “Adaptive” is shown, never the category.
Limits
  • Results appear here when USAW adds them to its rankings, which can lag a meet, especially international ones.
  • Totals made before August 1, 2026 were lifted in the old classes; they are checked against the 2027 class their bodyweight falls in.
  • Qualifying windows and event dates are still the ones entered from USAW's August 6, 2026 announcement. USAW's event pages are read daily for registration dates, but not every event has a page yet.
  • Age ranges used: Junior 15–20, U25 15–25, University 17–28, Youth 17 and under, Masters 35 and up.
