Qualifying tracker

Which PNW lifters have the total for a 2027 national event, and whose total is a legacy one: big enough, but made before the qualifying window opened, so it has to be done again.

 

  • Loading the rankings…
  • Qualified: the lifter's best ranked total meets the qualifying total for their class and was made inside this event's window.
  • Legacy total: the total is big enough, but it was made before the window opened, so it doesn't count for this event. USAW's rankings keep only each lifter's best total in a class, so a smaller total made later may already qualify them. Check their results before planning another meet.
  • Class and age group: the class is the one the lifter's bodyweight on the day falls in, and the age group uses the age they turn in the event's year. “All events” on a lifter shows the same check for every 2027 event.
  • This list is a planning aid, not an entry list. USAW confirms qualification at registration. University totals only apply to enrolled students, and adaptive totals aren't checked here.
Draft notes for the board
Data
Nothing is typed in by hand. Lifters, totals, bodyweights and dates come from data/rankings.json (USAW's public rankings, refreshed daily); totals and windows from data/qualifying-2027.json.
Limit
The rankings feed has each lifter's best total per class for the season, not every meet. A lifter shown as “legacy” or “short” may have another result that qualifies. Saving every result would remove this limit.
Assumptions to confirm
  • The age in the rankings is the lifter's birth-year age for the season, so next year's age is one more.
  • A lifter enters the class their bodyweight fell in when they made the total.
  • Age ranges used: Junior 15–20, U25 15–25, University 17–28, Youth 17 and under, Masters 35 and up.
  • The first and last days of a window count.
