2027 qualifying totals

The total needed in each weight class to enter USAW's 2027 national events. Filters work like the rankings page, and picking Youth or Masters shows each age group's own classes and totals. Adaptive totals are shown as USAW lists them, currently about half the standard total.

Draft notes for the board
Data
USAW's 2027 Qualifying Totals PDF, valid after Jan 1, 2027: 925 totals across Nationals, Series, Finals, Junior, U25, University, Youth and Masters Nationals. Updated when USAW posts a new version.
Limit
Qualifying periods differ by event. A total only counts if it was set inside that event's window.
Status
Waiting on the totals from USAW's PDF.
Note
The table uses USAW's labels: youth groups 16–17, 14–15, 12–13 and 11 & Under, and Series totals apply to both Series 1 and 2.
